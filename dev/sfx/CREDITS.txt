Star Fleet sound effects
========================
Taken from RUBICON PROTOCOL (https://github.com/tetblue/rubicon-protocol, MIT, (c) 2026 Loren Hsu),
which sliced, trimmed, normalized and re-encoded them as mono MP3 (scripts/sfx-prep.py there).
Only CC0 and CC-BY sources are used. The build packs this folder into web/assets/sfx.js (base64).

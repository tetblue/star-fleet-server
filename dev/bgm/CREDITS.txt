Star Fleet battle music
=======================
Music: Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
Source: https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/
Changes: a section of each track was cut into a seamless loop (3 s equal-power crossfade), normalized and
re-encoded to 80 kbps MP3 by RUBICON PROTOCOL's scripts/bgm-prep.py (https://github.com/tetblue/rubicon-protocol, MIT).

explore1.mp3 = "Lightless Dawn"        (no contact)
alert1.mp3   = "Crypto"                (fleets closing in)
combat1.mp3  = "Cyborg Ninja"          (engagement)
combat2.mp3  = "Volatile Reaction"     (engagement)

The build packs this folder into web/assets/bgm.js (base64), which the game downloads at startup.
